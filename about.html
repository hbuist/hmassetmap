About

This site is a prototype of an asset map for the Hintonburg—Mechanicsville neighbourhood. The idea is to have a compiled list of community programs, services, and businesses that can be used as a resource for various needs for community and personal development. According to the Ottawa Neighborhood Study (ONS, n.d.), in 2021, Hintonburg—Mechanicsville was home to just over 10,000 Ottawa residents, approximately 20% under the age of 24 and 14% over the age of 65. Of the population, 23% are racialized, 19% immigrants, and 5% Indigenous, First Nations, Métis and Inuit (ONS, n.d.). Approximately 66% are renters, 20% of which live in core housing need (ONS, n.d.), which is “housing that [is] unaffordable and/or fall[s] below acceptable housing standards” (Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation, 2019). Approximately 13% of residents are low-income measure after tax (LIM-AT), 27% of which are over the age of 65 (ONS, n.d.). My aim is to make the asset map available to and responding to all residents of the Hintonburg–Mechanicsville community, which would include those who are low income, unhoused, individuals, families, and older adults, while also addressing the unique needs of those who are racialized and/or immigrants.
